Finally. I managed to finish building the blog, which is also my development portfolio. It has taken me a long time, I have learned a lot and I still have many things to learn.

I welcome anyone who is wasting time reading my craziness. (Thanks).

The site is developed on the backend in Laravel and the frontend is built in Angular. Since it's my little corner on the web, I wanted to create it with one of the themes I like most: **Gruvbox**.

The style and distribution of objects on the page was inspired by Takuya Matsuya's web ([@craftzdog](https://www.craftz.dog/)).

The page logo, the coffee cup, I made it in Blender, with the idea of rendering it in 3D on the page. But achieving that would have taken me another year to finish. So I decided to render it in ASCII code with the help of Blender itself and the result is what you see on the page.

In the [Works](/en/trabajos) section there is a post dedicated to the development details of this blog/portfolio.
